Your experiment

Write one always and one never for how you'll show up in your test meeting.

If you chair it, propose one of each to the group and ask them to hold you to it. Ours, if you want to borrow them: We always start on time. You are always in choice. We never put comfort ahead of progress.

Lab Note: afterwards, reply to today's email with one line each: What did I do? What happened? What will I do differently next time?

Go deeper

Ground rules get nodded at and forgotten. Promises are something people can hold each other to, and that's what makes them stick.
