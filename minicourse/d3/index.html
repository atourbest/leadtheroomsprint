Ask for the Playback: check they heard what you meant

About 2 minutes
Your experiment

Next time you ask for something in a meeting, cut it to the fewest instructions that still work.

Then ask one person: “Can you play that back, so I know I was clear?” Notice the gap between what they say and what you meant.

Lab Note: afterwards, reply to today's email with one line each: What did I do? What happened? What will I do differently next time?

Go deeper

Slow is smooth, and smooth is fast. Most of us give poor instructions and believe we gave good ones. A ten-second playback costs less than a week of people doing the wrong thing.
