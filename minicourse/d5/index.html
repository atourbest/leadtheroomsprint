Your experiment

In the last three minutes of your test meeting, ask each person to say their own next action out loud: what, and by when.

Then open the next meeting with: “Last time we said… did we do it?”

Lab Note: afterwards, reply to today's email with one line each: What did I do? What happened? What will I do differently next time?

Go deeper

The fewer of your fingerprints on the action list, the more of it gets done. People keep promises they made out loud far better than tasks they were handed.
