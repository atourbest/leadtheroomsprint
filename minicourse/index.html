Free 5-day mini course

Anyone can learn to lead any room

Five days. Five 2-minute videos. Five small experiments you run in your real meetings, from before the room fills to after it empties. No title needed, and no 20 years in the room. Just one move a day that changes how the room responds to you.

  • Before the meeting: the 5-minute rehearsal actors use, and why rehearsing in your head doesn't count.
  • Opening the meeting: why ground rules fail, and the two promises that replace them.
  • During the meeting: how to check people heard your ask, and how to lift a flat meeting without being the boss.
  • Ending the meeting: the closing move that means actions actually happen afterwards.

Each day: one 2-minute video and one small experiment to run in a real meeting.
