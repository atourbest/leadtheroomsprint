Your experiment

Take the first 60 seconds of what you'll say in your test meeting. Read it out loud once.

Underline the 2–3 words you most need to land (your operative words) and mark one deliberate pause (a beat) with a slash. Then read it again and land them.

Lab Note: afterwards, reply to today's email with one line each: What did I do? What happened? What will I do differently next time?

Go deeper

It's not what you do in the room, it's what you do before it. Actors never learn a script in their heads, and neither should you. Saying it out loud shows you the words you trip on before the room does.
