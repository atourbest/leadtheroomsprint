Why This Session

The most important moments of your career happen in rooms.

High-stakes meetings, offsites, town halls, kick-offs. And most of us walk into them hoping today is a good day.

In this free 60-minute live session, Jason Moore and Michele Skogerboe — creators of the Lead the Room program, with 30+ years in the room with teams at Microsoft, Netflix, HSBC and Goldman Sachs across 36+ countries — show why influencing a room has nothing to do with being the loudest voice or the most senior person in it.
